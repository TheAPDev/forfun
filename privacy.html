Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

What we collect on this website

When you join the early-access list we collect your name, email address, and, if you choose to give them, your city and home type. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this website, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to tell you about Homeeni products, pricing and delivery dates. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Where it is stored

Early-access details are stored with our service providers on servers in India wherever possible, and are protected by access controls and encryption in transit.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Write to privacy@homeeni.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Homeeni devices and the app

A separate privacy notice will cover DoorCam and the Homeeni app before they are sold. Our commitments are that footage is stored locally by default, cloud backup is optional and hosted in India, and every sharing feature is opt-in.

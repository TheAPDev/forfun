Product 01 · DoorCam

The front door, made of one piece of black glass.

Every opening on DoorCam is reduced to a ring, a grille or a pin-hole. It looks like a design object on your wall, but it gives Homeeni Intelligence a clear view of everyone who comes to your door.

  • Jewellery-grade camera ring

    A satin-silver ring, Ø21 mm, stands 0.4 mm proud of the glass. Dark cover glass hides the lens.

  • Hidden motion sensor

    The PIR sensor sits behind 9 fine horizontal slits in an engraved Ø25 mm ring. It covers the full width of your doorway.

  • Halo button

    A Ø31 mm domed bell button inside an ice-blue light ring. Visitors find it in the dark.

  • One continuous face

    A 1.8 mm gloss-black fascia covers the whole front. No seam, no screw, no visible port.

  • Floating profile

    A crisp R1.6 front edge and a large R7 back curve make the 39 mm body look slimmer on the wall.

  • Two-way audio

    A built-in microphone and a Ø28 mm speaker let you talk to visitors, couriers and house help from anywhere.

Power

A battery that slides out, and a screw that keeps it in.

DoorCam runs on a LiFePO4 32700 cell, a battery type chosen for its stability in heat. It sits in a sled that slides out of the base. You charge it over USB-C, and the port is hidden against the wall once the sled is back in place.

One Torx security screw on the side locks the sled. Nobody can pull the battery off your door in a few seconds.

Questions

Before you ask.

What is Homeeni?

Homeeni is an end-to-end, AI-powered home security ecosystem built for Indian homes. Devices, AI, the app and storage are designed as one system. DoorCam is the first device.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Core safety features work without a subscription. Optional cloud backup, hosted in India, will be available for households that want it.

Where is my footage stored?

Footage is stored locally by default. If you switch on cloud backup, it is stored on servers in India. You decide what leaves your home.

When can I buy DoorCam?

DoorCam is in its final design stage. Pricing and delivery dates will be shared with the early-access list first.

Does DoorCam work in apartments?

Yes. DoorCam runs on its own battery and mounts on a wall plate, so it suits flats, gated societies and independent houses without new wiring.
